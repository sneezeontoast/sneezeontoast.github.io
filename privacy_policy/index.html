Celeb Slapper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 08/10/2025

Introduction

Celeb Slapper (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Short version: We do not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal information from users.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personal data or telemetry. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers.
  • Location data or device identifiers.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking.
  • Any files, photos, or content from your device.

All app functionality runs locally on your device; nothing is transmitted to our servers because we do not maintain user servers for this app.

2. Device Permissions

The app may request certain device permissions to enable specific features (for example: storage access, camera, or notifications). Any permission requested is used only locally on your device to enable the feature you explicitly choose to use.

We do not transmit, collect, or store data obtained via those permissions. If you deny a permission, the related feature will not function (only on your device).

3. Third-Party Services & Links

We do not use third-party analytics, ad networks, or trackers. If the app provides links to external websites or services, those external sites are outside our control and have their own privacy policies which you should review before use.

4. Children

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect any personal data, parents and guardians may rest assured that no child data is stored by us.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store user data, we have no user data to secure. If any future features introduce data collection, we will update this policy and implement reasonable security measures to protect any collected data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes that affect how user data is handled, we will update the “Last updated” date above and notify users through the app wherever practical.
